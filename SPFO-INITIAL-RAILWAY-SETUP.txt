SPFO INITIAL A/C PROBLEM - RAILWAY OPEN MAIL

Railway endpoint:
  /spfo-initial-mail/{CASE_ID}

Flow:
1. MMcasesBot detects SPFO Initial A/C Problem.
2. WhatsApp template sends OPEN MAIL button.
3. Button opens Railway form with Mariners Mentor logo.
4. Name + CDC No are read-only and loaded from Customer Profile.
5. Candidate enters Vessel Name, From, To. + Add Vessel supports more rows.
6. Open Mail prepares email to spfo-commr@spfo.gov.in.
7. Name and phone are loaded from Customer Profile.

Meta WhatsApp template required:
  Name: spfo_initial_mail_1
  Language: en
  Body parameter {{1}} = candidate name
  Website URL button must be DYNAMIC and end with {{1}}
  URL: https://YOUR-RAILWAY-DOMAIN/spfo-initial-mail/{{1}}

Optional application.properties:
  whatsapp.spfo.initial.template.name=spfo_initial_mail_1
  whatsapp.spfo.initial.template.language=en
  app.public.url=https://YOUR-RAILWAY-DOMAIN
